Orbis Mail privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What Orbis Mail is

Orbis Mail is a personal desktop email client that runs on the user's own Windows computer. It is used only by its owner.

What data it uses

When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, Orbis Mail accesses your email, labels or folders, and (if you connect it) your calendar, so that it can show, organise and send your mail. It uses this data only to provide those features to you.

Where the data goes

  • Your mail is downloaded directly from Google or Microsoft to your own computer and stored there.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager on your computer.
  • Orbis Mail has no servers. Your data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google user data

Orbis Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out of an account inside Orbis Mail to delete its stored sign-in, and delete the Orbis Mail folder on your computer to remove the local copy of your mail. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
